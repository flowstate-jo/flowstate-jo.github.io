FAQ

Questions & answers

Everything candidates usually ask about the Remote Sales Rep role. Tap a question to see the answer.

The role

Is the job fully remote?

Yes. This is a fully remote role — you work from home and don’t need to travel to an office.

What will I do day to day?

You’ll call US businesses, introduce our clients’ products, answer questions and help interested businesses move toward a sale. All our pitching is done by phone. You’ll work with a team lead and have weekly and monthly targets to hit.

What will I be selling?

We’ll tell you all about the product in your first interview. If you’re accepted, we then hold a second meeting where we go through the product and your training in detail.

Do I need sales experience?

Not necessarily. Experience in sales, customer service or a call centre helps, but what matters most is strong English, a positive attitude and the motivation to learn.

How good does my English need to be?

You’ll speak with US businesses every day, so you need to be comfortable holding a professional conversation in English. That’s why we ask for a short voice recording — it lets us hear how you naturally speak. It doesn’t have to be perfect; we’re listening for clear, confident English.

Is training provided?

Yes. New team members are trained on the product they’ll be selling and on our sales process before they start speaking to customers.

Hours, pay and equipment

What hours will I work?

There are no fixed working hours. The role is remote and paid on commission and bonuses, so you can work whenever suits you, as long as you hit your weekly and monthly targets.

Bear in mind that you’ll be calling US businesses, so your calls need to reach them during their business day.

Is this a full-time job?

Yes, this is a full-time position. There are no fixed hours, though: you set your own schedule and are measured on your weekly and monthly targets.

How is pay structured?

Pay is commission only: you earn a commission on the sales you make, and there is no fixed base salary. On top of your commission, we pay weekly and monthly bonuses. Top performers can earn 3,000+ JOD a month.

We’ll go through the full details with you during the interview.

What equipment do I need?

A reliable computer or laptop, a stable internet connection, a headset with a microphone and a quiet place to work. You’ll need to provide these yourself.

Do I need to live in Jordan?

No. We’re based in Amman, but this is a remote job, so anyone can apply.

Applying

How do I apply?

Fill in the application form. You’ll need your CV (PDF or Word) and a short voice recording of you reading a paragraph in English. It takes about five minutes.

How does the voice recording work?

The application form shows a short paragraph. Read it out loud and record yourself directly in your browser — you can listen back and record again as many times as you like. If you prefer, you can upload an audio file instead (MP3, M4A, WAV or WebM).

Tip: find a quiet room, hold your phone or microphone close, and speak at your natural pace.

The recorder isn’t working. What can I do?

First, make sure you choose “Allow” when your browser asks to use the microphone. If you opened this site inside an app such as Instagram or LinkedIn, open it in Chrome or Safari instead.

You can also record yourself with your phone’s voice recorder app and choose “Upload a file” on the form. Still stuck? Email austin@velarya.com.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews your application and listens to your recording. If your profile is a good match, we’ll contact you by email or phone to arrange an interview. We aim to get back to shortlisted candidates within 5 working days.

In the interview we’ll tell you about the product you’d be selling. If you’re accepted, a second meeting covers the product and your training in detail.

What do you do with my information?

We use your details, CV and voice recording only to review your application. If you’d like us to delete your information, email austin@velarya.com.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we’ll get back to you.
