About the role

As a Remote Sales Rep at Flow State, you’ll represent our clients’ products to businesses in the United States. You’ll call potential customers, find out what they need, pitch the product and help turn conversations into sales — all from home.

It’s a role for people who enjoy talking to others, speak English confidently and want to build a career in international sales.

What you’ll do

  • Call US businesses and introduce our clients’ products
  • Pitch clearly and confidently over the phone — all our pitching is done by calls
  • Ask good questions to understand what each business needs
  • Follow up with interested prospects and move them toward a sale
  • Keep clear notes on your calls and progress
  • Hit your weekly and monthly sales targets, with support from your team lead

What we’re looking for

  • Strong spoken English — you’ll be on calls with US businesses every day
  • Self-motivated — with no fixed hours, you manage your own time
  • A confident, friendly way of speaking and the motivation to learn
  • Your own computer, a headset with a microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to work
  • Sales, customer service or call-centre experience is a plus, but not required
  • Aged 18 or over

What we offer

  • A fully remote role — work from home, wherever you live
  • No fixed working hours — work whenever suits you, as long as you hit your targets
  • Commission-only pay, plus weekly and monthly bonuses: you earn on the sales you make, and top performers can earn 3,000+ JOD a month
  • Training on the product you’ll sell and on our sales process
  • Daily experience with the US market and US business culture
  • Support from a team lead as you work toward your targets
